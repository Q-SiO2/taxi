# Welcome illustration provenance and legacy licences

## Active welcome portrait — revised 2026-10-04

The active master is `source/illustration_landing_navy_outline_master.png`.
It was created with the built-in image-generation tool at the owner's explicit
request after the owner rejected the vector attempt. The later request was for
a phone-friendly portrait and substantially less dense linework. The latest 941x1672
RGB PNG is mirrored unchanged into exports and Compose resources; no manual
crop, stretch, recolour or artistic post-processing was applied. The previous
4:5 PNG is retained as `source/illustration_landing_navy_outline_4x5_legacy.png`.
Exact final
edit instructions and the generation sequence are in `prompts.md`. No model
version was exposed by the tool, so none is claimed.

Al-Qarawiyyin's architecture was researched through the
[AKDN rehabilitation project](https://the.akdn/en/how-we-work/our-agencies/aga-khan-trust-culture/akaa/rehabilitation-al-qaraouiyine-mosque)
and the Moroccan tourism office's Fez brochure. These were architectural
references only: no source photograph or unDraw asset was supplied to the image
model, copied into the generated master, or bundled as third-party photography.
The scene is stylised and inspired by the square minaret, pitched pavilion roof
and courtyard arches; it is not an architectural survey, a map, a claim that taxis
enter the sacred courtyard, or endorsement by the mosque/institution.

The selected visual is decorative. It must never stand in for assignment,
navigation, availability, booking or payment state. Final owner approval and
representative-phone visual acceptance remain separate from file delivery.
