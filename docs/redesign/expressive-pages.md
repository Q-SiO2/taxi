# Decoration and motion assignment for every view

**Proposed 14 October 2026; owner approval pending.** Every existing design ID has a placement decision. A blank-art decision is intentional, not an omitted page. Exact illustration/motion recipes are in [expressive-motion.md](expressive-motion.md), editable masters in [the art manifest](expressive-assets.json), and all variants in the [visual motion board](expressive-board.html).

Decorative geometry is not a replacement for a real map, record, payment, driver identity, credential or route. Entry motion leaves text and controls immediately available. Reduce-motion, hidden/offscreen and active-driver contexts have static fallbacks.
