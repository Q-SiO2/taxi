**Proposed; starts only after owner confirmation.** Full design coverage and implementation order are separate. All 213 compositions are reviewable now; UX-1 establishes a shared foundation for every delivered surface. UX-2–UX-6 execute core and participant/representative console flows; UX-8 completes secondary editor/workflow polish. UX-7 validates all alpha-reachable pages and explicitly discloses remaining secondary acceptance. Deferred rows do not authorize unsupported backend work.

A view inherited by multiple roles is assigned once and reused; no duplicate app implementation. R42 is development-only and excluded from the presentation/release navigation. Scope-specific/capability-gated views remain unavailable until their real contracts are accepted.
